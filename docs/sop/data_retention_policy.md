# Data Retention Policy

## Customer data

Retain while account/order support is active and as required by law.

## Orders and payments

Retain according to accounting and legal requirements.

## Support tickets

Retain for operational history and dispute resolution.

## Privacy requests

Process export/delete requests through `/api/privacy`.

## Logs

Operational logs should be rotated and retained according to infrastructure policy.

## Deletion

Before deletion, verify:

- legal retention obligations;
- unresolved orders/refunds;
- fraud/risk flags;
- accounting requirements.

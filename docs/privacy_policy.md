
# Privacy Policy

FLASHIN Mini App values your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Information We Collect
- Personal information (e.g. name, phone number, Telegram username) required for order processing.
- Payment details handled by third-party payment providers (we do not store payment card numbers).
- Analytics data collected with your consent to improve our services.

## How We Use Information
- Fulfil and manage orders.
- Provide customer support.
- Send transactional messages via Telegram.
- With your consent, send promotional content and newsletters.

## Your Rights
- Access, modify, or delete your personal data.
- Withdraw consent at any time.
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Contact us at privacy@flashin.example for any privacy concerns.
